TwyLapse Support

Questions, problems and requests: support@laxei.app
We answer in English or Japanese, usually within a few days.

Before you write

The manual explains what each message in the App means, and which power-saving settings to check if a shoot stops part-way through the night.

What to include

  • the App version (shown at the bottom of the menu)
  • your phone model and Android version
  • the camera model and its firmware version, or the external device model
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened, with the date and time
  • if possible, the debug log: Debug log → Fetch logs saves it to the Download folder (see the manual, chapter 9). Attach that file. Logs can contain the names of your plans and places; remove anything you would rather not send.

Purchases and refunds

  • New phone? Sign in with the same Google account and open Menu → Add-ons; your purchases are restored once the phone is online.
  • Refunds: within 48 hours of purchase you can request one yourself through Google Play (how to request a refund). See the terms of use.
