Privacy Policy for TwyLapse

Effective date: TODO: date of publication (first published September 28, 2026)

laxei.app (“we”) provides the Android application TwyLapse (the “App”), which controls cameras to record time-lapse sequences. This policy explains what information the App handles, what reaches us, what reaches other services, and how you can control it.

1. Summary

  • Collection of usage and crash information is optional, and nothing is collected until you agree to it. Declining limits no feature.
  • We never receive your photographs, the dates on which you shoot, your coordinates, or any text you enter.
  • The free version shows advertisements delivered by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked before personalized advertising is used, and you can change your choice at any time. The paid add-ons remove advertisements.
  • Some features contact other services directly from your phone: displaying the map, and downloading the camera catalog and device software. Section 5 lists exactly what they receive.
  • We do not sell your information.

2. Who we are

The App is provided by laxei.app, a sole proprietorship operated by Yoshikazu Eto. Our postal address is disclosed without delay on request. We are the “controller” of the information described in section 3 and the business handling personal information under Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information. Contact: support@laxei.app.

3. Information we collect, with your consent

When you first launch the App, it asks whether it may send quality-improvement information. If you agree, the following is collected through Google’s Firebase services (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics):

App activity

  • the start and the end of a time-lapse session
  • the camera model, the phone and external device models, and the App version
  • the interval between frames, the number of frames, and the duration of the session
  • how the session ended (completed, stopped by you, or error), and a numeric code identifying the error
  • the time of day at which the session ended — hours and minutes only, never the date

Crash and diagnostic information

  • reports produced when the App stops unexpectedly: where the failure occurred, a short history of the screens and operations immediately beforehand, whether a session was running, and counts and types of the devices in use (for example, the number of plans and external devices, the type of connection, and the external device’s software version)

Identifiers and approximate location

  • an identifier that Firebase assigns to your installation of the App. It is specific to this App and is discarded when you uninstall it.
  • an approximate country and region, which Google derives from the IP address of the connection

4. Advertising

The free version of the App shows a banner at the bottom of the screen, delivered by Google AdMob. No advertisement is shown while a time-lapse session is running, and none at all once you have bought either add-on.

  • To select, deliver and measure advertisements and to prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may collect your device’s advertising identifier, your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), information about your device and the App, and your interactions with the advertisements.
  • Google acts as an independent controller of that information. Its handling is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before advertising cookies or identifiers are used, through a consent form provided by Google. If you do not consent, only non-personalized or limited advertising is shown.
  • In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising.
  • Wherever you are, you can review or change your choice at any time under Menu → Permissions and settings → “Ad personalization” TODO: confirm the menu label once implemented. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Information collected for advertising is separate from the quality-improvement information in section 3; agreeing to one is not agreeing to the other.

5. Information sent to other services when you use certain features

The following connections are made directly from your phone to the services named. We do not receive the information. Each service handles it under its own privacy policy.

  • Map and your location — when you pick a location on the map, map images are loaded from a map service, currently the OpenStreetMap Foundation’s tile servers. The map opens around the shooting location already set, so the map service receives the area around that location — in effect, its approximate position — as well as the areas you move to and your IP address. If you would rather not share this, set the location by typing or pasting coordinates instead of using the map. If we change the map service, we will update this policy. (OSMF privacy policy)
  • Camera catalog and device software — the list of cameras and lenses, and the software written to external devices, are downloaded from GitHub, which receives your IP address. (GitHub privacy statement)
  • QR codes — the setup code of an external device is read with Google’s code scanner in Google Play services. The image is processed on the phone and is not given to the App or to us; Google may collect diagnostic information about the scanner.
  • Purchases — add-ons are sold through Google Play, which handles payment. We receive only whether you own an add-on and the order details Google Play provides to developers; we never see your payment details.

6. Information we do not collect

  • the photographs and videos you record
  • the date of a session, and the latitude, longitude or name of your shooting location
  • the names of shooting plans, places and devices, Wi‑Fi network names (SSIDs) and passwords, camera serial numbers and MAC addresses, and any other text you enter or configure
  • your name, e‑mail address, telephone number, or any account credentials (the App has no accounts)

Dates and coordinates are excluded deliberately. The App’s schedule follows sunrise, sunset and twilight, which means that the latitude of a shooting location can be inferred from the times of twilight across the seasons. Reporting only the time of day tells us what we need without revealing where you were.

7. Information kept on your device (not sent to us)

The following is required for the App to work. It is stored on your device, or exchanged only with your own camera and external device over your local network or Bluetooth, and is not transmitted to us. When you use the map, however, the map service described in section 5 learns the approximate area of the shooting place.

  • the coordinates of your shooting locations, obtained from your device’s location services or entered by you (never sent to us; only the map service in section 5 learns the approximate area when you use the map)
  • the photographs and videos the App records, saved to Pictures/TwyLapse and Movies/TwyLapse on your device
  • shooting plans, your registered equipment, external device settings, and Wi‑Fi and camera credentials (credentials are stored in encrypted form)
  • a random identifier the App creates so that your external devices can recognize your phone
  • whether you own an add-on, and how many trial sessions you have used
  • operation logs. These are never transmitted unless you export and send them yourself.

The permissions the App requests — camera, location, nearby devices and notifications — are used solely to provide these features. Location is used only while you are using the App.

8. Why we use information, and our legal bases

  • Quality improvement (section 3): to identify and fix defects and to understand how the App is used — on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Advertising (section 4): to fund the free version — on the basis of your consent where the law requires it, and otherwise our legitimate interest in offering the App free of charge.
  • Features (section 5): to provide the feature you are using — necessary to provide the App you asked for.
  • Messages you send us (section 13): to answer you — our legitimate interest in responding, or steps you ask us to take.

We do not use information to identify you personally, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

9. Your choices

  • Quality-improvement information: change it at any time under Menu → Permissions and settings → “Send quality-improvement information” (in the Japanese interface: メニュー → スマホ権限、設定 → 品質向上のための情報を送る).
  • Advertising: see section 4.
  • Location: you can refuse the location permission and enter locations by hand or pick them on the map.

10. Service providers and transfers abroad

We use Google LLC’s Firebase as a service provider to process the information described in section 3 on our behalf. Information may be stored on servers outside your country, including in the United States. For transfers from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google relies on safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Under Japanese law, the United States is a foreign country whose system of personal-information protection differs from Japan’s; Google has put in place measures equivalent to those required of businesses in Japan.

We do not sell your information, nor provide it to third parties for their own purposes, other than as described for advertising in section 4.

11. How long information is kept

  • usage information: 14 months at most
  • crash and diagnostic information: 90 days
  • advertising information: as determined by Google (see section 4)
  • e‑mail you send us: as long as needed to answer you

After that period the information is deleted automatically.

12. Requesting deletion

If you would like the information collected from your device to be deleted, please write to support@laxei.app. So that we can find the right records, please include the identifier shown under Menu → Permissions and settings TODO: show the Firebase installation ID in the app, and name the item here. 13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect what was done before). Under Japanese law you may request disclosure, correction, suspension of use or deletion of retained personal data. To use any of these rights, write to support@laxei.app; we will answer within one month.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other US states: we do not sell personal information for money. Allowing Google to use your advertising identifier for personalized advertising may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising; you can opt out as described in section 4. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

14. Information you send to us directly

If you contact us, the e‑mail address, name and any other details you provide (including logs you choose to attach) are used solely to respond to you. They are kept only as long as is necessary for that purpose, and are not provided to third parties.

15. Security

Information sent to Firebase and Google is encrypted in transit. Communication with your own camera on your local network uses the protocol the camera supports, which may not be encrypted. We keep the information we receive to a minimum.

16. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for consenting to online services in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

17. Changes to this policy

If the information we collect or the purposes for which we use it change, we will revise this policy and update the effective date above. Where a change is significant, the App will ask for your agreement again.
