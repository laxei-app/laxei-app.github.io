Why a whole night is hard

From sunset to deep night the light falls by more than twenty stops, and it climbs back again before sunrise. Shooting that as one time-lapse — the “holy grail” — means changing the exposure all night, slowly enough that the video does not flicker.

The interval timer built into most cameras rides on the camera’s auto exposure. Once the sky is too dark to meter, the auto exposure has nothing to follow, and the sequence breaks somewhere in the twilight.

TwyLapse works the other way round. It knows where you are and when the sun sets and rises, so it plans the night in advance: metered auto exposure while there is light to measure, a fixed night exposure while there is not, and a measured glide between the two.

What it does

Plans the night for you

Enter the start and end time and the location. TwyLapse works out sunset, twilight, night and sunrise, and builds the schedule.

Exposure that follows the sky

Sunset, night, sunrise and daytime each have their own exposure rules. Between them it moves ⅓ stop at a time, so nothing jumps.

Little flicker

Exposure smoothing (hysteresis and a gentle ramp) ignores small wobbles in the light but still follows real changes.

You choose what moves

Set limits for ISO, aperture and shutter speed, and the order they move in — keep noise low, or keep the aperture fixed.

See it before you go

The schedule shows the twilight by sun altitude, with moonrise and moonset. The simulation shows what will be in the frame.

A report after every shoot

Each shoot leaves a capture report: frames on time, metering, exposure — and advice, such as “a longer interval will be more stable”.

Three ways to shoot

Free

Phone camera

Fix the phone to a tripod and start. The app builds the video for you, and can also keep DNG or JPEG frames.

Add-on

Phone + your camera

External device

What you need

  • An Android phone running Android 8.0 or later.
  • A tripod, and for a long night, a power bank for the phone.
  • For the external camera add-on: a Canon camera that supports CCAPI over Wi-Fi. list of tested models
  • For the external device add-on: an M5Stack CoreS3 or M5StickS3, bought separately. The app writes the software to it over USB.

Try before you buy. How well a camera works with TwyLapse depends on the model. Use the trial with the camera you plan to use before buying the add-on.

Power saving on some phones. Some manufacturers add their own power saving that can stop a background shoot part-way through. The manual explains the settings to check.

Your data

Your photographs, your locations and the dates you shot stay on your phone. With your consent, the app sends crash records and usage statistics to help us improve it — you can say no, and nothing in the app changes. Details: Privacy policy.
